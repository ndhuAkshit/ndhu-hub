Slide 2 • Problem Statement

Three Everyday Campus Pain Points

Why Dong Hwa Students Need a Dedicated, Mobile-First Mutual Aid Network

🛵

Highway Scooter Breakdowns

Daily student commuting across Highway 9 and 11-Bing. Flat tires, punctured treads, or dead batteries leave riders stranded miles from town garages.

⚠️ Pain: No instant peer assistance channel
🌧️

Valley Climate & 90%+ Humidity

Sudden afternoon downpours catch students without umbrellas. Relative humidity exceeds 85–95%, causing mold damage and biting midges (Forcipomyia taiwana).

⚠️ Pain: Steep freshman survival learning curve
💬

Fragmented Knowledge & Chats

Dining deals, add/drop registration etiquette, and course lottery weighting algorithms remain lost inside dying Facebook posts and noisy LINE groups.

⚠️ Pain: Information evaporates every semester
Solution: NDHU Hub turns individual struggles into peer community solutions. Slide 2 / 6
